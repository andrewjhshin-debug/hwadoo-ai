# Fourth and fifth singing-bowl skins

Existing first three choices remain in place. Fourth is `ivory` / 크림빛, fifth is `hammered` / 두드린 놋쇠. Both use the existing skin picker, saved preference, preload loop, click/sound behaviour and normal/expanded bowl renderers.

Artwork was created with built-in ImageGen. The original bowl was a camera/footprint guide; the user's monk/bowl photograph was the fifth material reference only. No browser Gemini session was needed.

Final fourth prompt specification: keep a broad low round bowl and elliptical opening at the original camera angle; make the bowl fully opaque milky ivory/cream-yellow frosted quartz/ceramic with dense matte satin walls and a thick clean rim. No translucency, internal glow or gold metal. Replace pink cushion with a quiet mint-grey round woven linen support, and old round wooden striker with a pale ash straight handle and white cylindrical felt head at lower right. Soft premium app-object rendering, upper-left light, transparent background, no text or decoration.

Final fifth prompt specification: traditional hand-hammered Korean brass/bronze singing bowl, irregular broad hammer dimples, warm honey-bronze reflections and darker burnished brown lower patina, thin polished rim and visible metallic interior. Low charcoal-grey braided support ring with pale grey woven knots, dark walnut striker with wine-red felt head at lower right. Reference photo supplies material only: no monk, microphone, stand, table or other scene objects. Soft realistic 3D rendering, original approximate footprint/camera, transparent background and generous margins.

Project originals are `design/singing-bowls/bowl-ivory-v1-source.png` and `bowl-hammered-v1-source.png`. `scripts/prepare-bowl-skin.mjs` only isolates major alpha components, crops/resizes without stretching, and registers them to the original 841×716 display canvas. Prepared WebP sprites are `public/obj/bowl-ivory-v1.webp` and `bowl-hammered-v1.webp`. Comparison: `design/singing-bowls/ivory-hammered-preview-v1.png`.

Validation checks skin order/IDs, unchanged original three choices, original canvas dimensions, alpha transparency outside and opaque bowl walls, and shared main/full-screen renderers. Production build validates integration. No new instrument synthesis or claim of physical-phone visual verification.
